What the data support

Identity is transferred causally from the visual reference, while the base model remains responsible for scheduler steps. The result is strong but not free: scene CLIP decreases by 0.0090 and the blind audit finds a 3.9% visible-defect tail.

Why trust regions matter

Removing projection worsens CCIP from 0.0554 to 0.1289 on the locked subset. Raw score magnitude is similarly unstable. One inner update is weaker than three same-state reevaluations.

What remains unsolved

When the editor itself omits a cue, reconstruction fields can restore appearance only by importing layout. Channel-energy transport avoids gross copying but cannot reliably recover the missing identity cue.
